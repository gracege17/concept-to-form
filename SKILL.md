# Concept to Form

**From meaning to form. From form to system.**

An independent design workflow inspired by the wider tradition of concept-driven graphic design. Not affiliated with Pentagram.

## The five questions

1. **UNDERSTAND — What is the real communication problem?** 搞清楚：到底要解决什么问题？
2. **CREATIVE CONCEPT — What is the one meaningful idea?** 想明白：最值得表达的一个想法是什么？
3. **VISUAL PRINCIPLE — How can the idea become visible?** 想办法：怎么让想法变得看得见？
4. **GRAPHIC SYSTEM — What rules make the idea repeatable?** 定规则：怎么让这个视觉想法持续成立？
5. **DESIGN CRITIQUE — Does every decision serve the idea?** 做判断：每个元素真的有必要吗？

## How to run

Determine the user's intent: discovery, concept development, visual exploration, system building, critique, or precise edit. Start at the appropriate step. Do **not** restart a project when its brief, decisions or reference imagery is already provided.

### 01 Understand — Research → Collect observations

- Separate **client facts**, **verified external research**, **designer interpretations** and **open questions**. Never label your inference as a client's preference.
- Identify the audience, communication objective, desired emotional response, required content, format, deadline, brand constraints and known dislikes.
- Review source material, actual context and real behaviors before gathering aesthetic references.
- Produce a one-paragraph problem statement, a short evidence-backed brief and 5–10 specific observations.
- Avoid pretending to have read files or seen images that are unavailable.
- Template: [01-discovery.md](templates/01-discovery.md).
- **Gate:** Can you explain what the audience must understand, feel or do, and what makes this situation distinctive?

### 02 Creative Concept — Find a tension → Define an idea

- Seek a particular truth, meaningful contrast, relationship, or unexpected observation. A tension need not be conflict.
- Create 3 genuinely distinct **concept territories**, each containing: evidence/insight, tension, central idea, why it belongs to this client, audience effect, risks.
- Express each concept in one sentence. An object (chair, megaphone), medium (risograph) or aesthetic (minimalism) is **not** itself a concept.
- Do not automatically impose a clever metaphor when straightforward communication is better.
- Template: [02-concept-cards.md](templates/02-concept-cards.md).
- **Gate:** Can you explain why this idea is particular to the brief rather than applicable to any organization?

### 03 Visual Principle — Explore relationships → Sketch

- Map a selected idea to observable **visual behavior**, e.g. one element creates space for another; separate forms share a load; a small mark reorders a larger system.
- Explore operations: scale, connect, overlap, subtract, repeat, reframe, balance, transform. See [visual-operations.md](references/visual-operations.md).
- Generate at least 3 substantially different mechanisms before deciding on symbols or objects. Explain the meaning and possible misreading of each.
- Sketch in monochrome first. Test 6–9 small thumbnail compositions; isolate changes in scale, spatial relationship, hierarchy and negative space.
- Avoid extra symbols, literal charity clichés and ornamental explanation.
- Template: [03-visual-explorations.md](templates/03-visual-explorations.md).
- **Gate:** Is the primary idea visible when color, texture and decorative details are removed?

### 04 Graphic System — Define rules → Execute and test

- Define a compact grammar for layout/grid, typography hierarchy, recurring forms, palette, texture, imagery and exclusions.
- Label **fixed**, **variable** and **forbidden** properties.
- Apply the same idea to at least 2–3 real formats, not just a hero mockup.
- Only now generate styled images if the user requested them. Preserve verified copy, constraints and aspect ratios; distinguish concept art from production assets.
- Do not invent dates, brand colors, logos, measurements or endorsements. For exact printed output, verify dimensions, bleed, legibility and correct copy separately.
- Template: [04-visual-system.md](templates/04-visual-system.md).
- **Gate:** Does the idea retain its identity across sizes and placements without accumulating decorations?

### 05 Design Critique — Evaluate → Refine → Iterate

- Judge **concept relevance, communication, originality, focal point, hierarchy, accessibility, craft and system potential**.
- Run an **element audit**: What does it mean? What compositional job does it do? What happens if removed? Meaning or an indispensable formal function may justify an element. Do not force every pixel to be a metaphor.
- Categorize each issue before editing: **problem definition, concept, visual mechanism, composition, system, or execution**.
- Offer specific **keep / remove / change** decisions and rationale.
- When revising an image, preserve all non-target choices. Avoid an entirely new composition when the user requests one local edit.
- Compare variants against the same brief. Record decisions and unresolved questions.
- Template: [05-critique.md](templates/05-critique.md); [critique-checklist.md](references/critique-checklist.md).
- **Gate:** Are the remaining decisions intentional and traceable to the concept or necessary communication?

## Default deliverables and conversation behavior

- Start with a **compact answer relevant to the current stage**, not a massive report by default.
- Identify the next useful action and output a usable artifact: discovery board, concept cards, mechanism sketches, system sheet or critique log.
- Ask a clarifying question only when indispensable; otherwise state assumptions and progress.
- Do not generate a final polished image during early discovery unless explicitly asked.
- When generating variants, change **one major variable at a time** unless comparing distinct concepts.
- Prioritize clarity over decorative sophistication. Aesthetic craft matters but does not excuse weak meaning.
- Cite/attribute external research and maintain client confidentiality in public examples.
